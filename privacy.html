Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Data Collection

Furbesty collects information essential to providing our luxury residence social club services, including your name, unit number, pet details, and social interactions within your building.

2. Use of Information

Your data is used solely to facilitate neighbor connections, playdates, and group walks. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Visibility & Control

You maintain full control over your profile visibility. You can toggle "Invisible Mode" to hide your status from neighbors and adjust messaging preferences at any time.

4. Data Retention

You may request account deletion at any time through the app settings. Upon deletion, all personal data, pet profiles, and social history will be permanently removed from our systems.

5. Editorial Oversight

To maintain the high standards of our club, content is subject to editorial review. Reported content or users may be moderated to ensure a safe and respectful environment.
